     1→     1→# Worklog — Prism Social (Social Media Team Work Management Platform)
     2→     2→
     3→     3→Project: Apple macOS-27 "liquid glass" inspired social media management platform.
     4→     4→Goal: Beat Hootsuite / Later / Sprout Social with advanced multi-account publishing, AI per-account content generation, team workflow, and deep analytics.
     5→     5→
     6→     6→## Architecture decisions
     7→     7→- Single `/` route; client-side view switching via Zustand store (app-shell).
     8→     8→- Prisma + SQLite. Seed script at `prisma/seed.ts`.
     9→     9→- API routes under `src/app/api/*`.
    10→    10→- z-ai-web-dev-sdk (LLM) for per-account AI caption generation.
    11→    11→- Design system: "liquid glass" (backdrop-blur, translucent layered surfaces, vibrant retina colors, rounded geometry, soft shadows). No Apple logos/icons copied.
